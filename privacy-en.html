Privacy Policy

App Destápalas (com.jrsoft.solitario) · Last updated: 6 November 2026

In one sentence: Destápalas keeps your games, your settings and your statistics on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers of our own, and none of that leaves the phone unless you share the app or save a backup. Apart from that, only the technical and advertising data collected by Google’s ads library leave it, as explained below.

Who is responsible

The data controller is JR Soft, the trade name of Javier Román Sáez, developer of the Destápalas app. You can get in touch at demual@gmail.com.

What this app does NOT do

  • No sign-up, account, user name or password. You are never asked for an email address, a phone number, a name or your location.
  • It does not upload your games. Games in progress, daily challenges, your streak, statistics and settings are stored on your phone. This app has no servers of its own.
  • It includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics). Google’s ads library does send its own technical data, explained below.
  • We do not sell or rent your data. To show and measure ads, Google’s ads SDK (AdMob) collects device identifiers, as explained in this policy. Some US privacy laws may consider that “selling” or “sharing” personal data (see “US residents”).
  • It does not ask for permission to use the microphone, the camera, your files, your contacts or your location. Backup files are saved and opened with Android’s own screens, and only the ones you choose.
  • No purchases, coins, loot boxes or betting. The game is free, and game points are worth nothing outside the game: they cannot be bought, exchanged or used to win prizes.
  • Your data is not included in Android’s backup to Google’s cloud.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state and advertising ID (AD_ID and the Android advertising services permissions), needed for advertising (see below).
  • The ones Google’s libraries add on their own (WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE), which give no access to any of your data.
  • Vibration and sounds need no permission. So as not to make sounds when the phone is silenced, the app asks Android whether the ringer is on silent or vibrate; that answer does not leave the phone.

Data that stays on your phone only

This information is kept in the app’s private storage. It disappears if you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android’s settings.

  • The game in progress of each of the ten games and today’s daily challenge, with their moves (so they can be undone) and their time.
  • Your statistics for each game and mode: games, wins, streaks and best records (time, moves and points) and, if you play with that scoring, the cumulative points total.
  • The daily challenges you have completed in each game and your streak of days.
  • Your settings: the chosen game, how cards are drawn and scored, “always winnable deals”, Spider suits, relaxed mode, the timer, controls (tapping, left-handed mode), appearance (colours, number size, card back, table, animations), sound and vibration.
  • Which deals from the bank of winnable deals you have already had, so they are not repeated.
  • The date the app last asked you for a rating, so it does not ask again too soon.

Backups and changing phones

Android’s cloud backup (the one in your Google account) is turned off for this app’s data: none of it is uploaded. Android does keep in your account the list of apps you had installed, to reinstall them, but that list is managed by Google and carries no app data.

If, when setting up a new phone, you copy your data directly from the old one, with or without a cable, the app’s data may move to the new phone. You request that transfer and Android carries it out, with its own tool or the phone maker’s, under their own terms. According to Android’s documentation, it goes from one phone to the other without passing through servers. In no case does it pass through ours: JR Soft receives nothing.

Whether it happens depends on the Android version and the phone maker (it does not happen on Android 8 or earlier), so we cannot guarantee it. If you set up the new phone from your Google account backup, without the old phone, this app’s data does not come back, because it is not in that backup. To avoid depending on that, you can save a backup to a file yourself (see below). Your choice about ads is not transferred: on the new phone the app will ask you again.

What you choose to share

  • Sharing the app. “Share the app” (Settings → About) hands a text with the link to Destápalas on Google Play to the app you choose on Android’s share screen (messaging, social networks, email…). The link carries a parameter that only says it comes from sharing the app, with none of your data. From then on that app handles it, under its own terms.
  • Backup to a file. “Save a backup file” (Settings → Backup) saves a file (destapalas-YYYY-MM-DD.json) with your settings, statistics, challenges, streak and games in progress, wherever you choose, using Android’s screen. If that folder syncs with a cloud (for example Google Drive), the service you chose does it, under its own terms. “Restore a backup” reads the file you choose, only if it is a Destápalas backup, and first warns you what will be replaced.
  • Writing to us. If you write to the contact address, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us. We use it only to reply and delete the message once the matter is resolved and, at the latest, one year after receiving it.

Data that does leave the phone

Advertising: Google AdMob

The app is free and is supported by a discreet Google AdMob ad (a banner, muted) that only appears at the foot of the home, statistics and daily challenge screens: never during a game, when you win, in the settings or full screen. Ad requests carry nothing about your games.

As in any app with AdMob, Google’s SDK collects Android’s advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads), the app set ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), interactions with the app and with ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses them to serve and measure ads, for statistics and to prevent fraud, under its own policy. The app asks Google for ads suitable at most for teens.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before serving personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline, you will see non-personalised ads (or limited ads, without identifiers, if you also decline the use of storage) and the app works exactly the same.

The ads consent message is shown by Google with a text common to all its advertising partners, which is why it mentions precise geolocation. According to Google, its ads SDK only uses the approximate location inferred from the IP address, and this app passes it no location. You can decline that use in “Manage options”.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ratings and Google Play

From time to time, after you win a game (from the third win onwards and at most once every four months), the app may ask you for a rating with Google Play’s official window, which Google manages. “Rate the app” and “More apps by JR Soft” open Google Play. To show you only the apps available in your country, “More apps by JR Soft” looks on the phone at the SIM card’s country or the system region; that information does not leave the phone. Its links to Google Play carry a parameter that only says they come from Destápalas, with none of your data.

IP address

As with any app that connects to the internet, the Google services mentioned receive your IP address when connecting. This app has no servers of its own, so it keeps no logs of any kind.

Legal basis and retention

Legal basis. What is processed on your phone never reaches JR Soft: your games and statistics are stored so the game does what you ask of it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For what does leave it:

  • Personalised advertising and use of device storage for advertising purposes in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: your consent, which you give or refuse in Google’s form and can withdraw in Settings → Ad privacy options (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE).
  • Non-personalised advertising, its measurement, technical logs and fraud prevention: the legitimate interest in funding a free app, keeping it working well and protecting it from fraud (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • If you write to us: the legitimate interest in replying and resolving what you tell us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Google processes AdMob data as an independent controller, under its own policies.

Data stored on your phone stays there until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data. This app keeps no data outside your phone, except the messages you send us, which are deleted once the matter is resolved and, at the latest, one year after receiving them; Google keeps its own according to its policies.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union, the GDPR and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD) give you the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. In practice, this app keeps none of your data outside the phone except the messages you send us (for those, write to the contact address); AdMob data is processed by Google, with whom you can exercise your rights (policies.google.com/privacy). For what is on your phone:

  • Access and portability: save a backup to a file from Settings → Backup.
  • Erasure: clear the app’s data in Android’s settings or uninstall it. There is no need to ask anyone.
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent: change your ads choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.

You can also write to the contact address or lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or with the data protection authority of your country.

US residents

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you have the right to ask that your personal data not be sold or shared for targeted advertising. You can do it in the app: Settings → Ad privacy options → “Don’t sell or share my data”. From then on you will only see non-personalised ads. The app sends no sensitive data to anyone; your games stay on your phone. For ads, Google’s SDK uses device identifiers and an approximate location (city level). For any other request about your data, write to us at demual@gmail.com.

Residents of Brazil

If you live in Brazil, the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) gives you the rights of confirmation and access, correction, anonymisation, blocking or deletion, portability, information about whom your data is shared with and about the possibility of not giving consent, and withdrawal of consent (Art. 18). The legal bases are those described in “Legal basis and retention”: your consent for personalised advertising (Art. 7, I) and legitimate interest for the rest (Art. 7, IX). AdMob data may be processed outside Brazil, with the safeguards described in “International transfers” (Art. 33). As everywhere else, this app keeps none of your data outside the phone except the messages you send us; for any request, write to us at demual@gmail.com. You can also complain to the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

Children

Destápalas is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16; the technical data AdMob sends is described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes substantially, the new version will be published at this same address with a different update date. You can always open it from Settings → About → Privacy policy.
